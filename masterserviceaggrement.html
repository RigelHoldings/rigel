Master Service Aggrement

Last updated: May 5, 2025


1. Introduction

This Master Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on 10 December 2021 (“Effective Date”) by and between Rigel Enterprises, having its office at Building No. 96/240, Neelwal, New Delhi – 110041, acting through its authorized representative Mr. Nipun Drall, Owner & CEO (hereinafter referred to as the “Company,” “Rigel Enterprises,” “We,” “Us,” or “Service Provider”), and ________________________________, having its address at ____________________________________________ (hereinafter referred to as the “Client,” “You,” or “Member”). The Company and the Client may individually be referred to as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”


2. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms and conditions governing the services and business support provided by Rigel Enterprises to the Client in connection with marketplace seller account registration, product listing, order processing, product procurement, dispatch coordination, customer returns, and related services. This Agreement is intended to establish a clear understanding between the Parties regarding their respective responsibilities, payment obligations, order-processing procedures, product pricing, dispatch arrangements, confidentiality, and other matters relating to the business relationship. The services provided by the Company shall remain subject to the applicable requirements, policies, procedures, and operational conditions of the relevant online marketplaces and other third-party service providers involved in the transaction.



3. DOCUMENTATION AND ACCOUNT REGISTRATION

3.1. The Company may collect documents and information from the Client through its designated Google Form or through such other method as may be communicated by the Company from time to time.
3.2. Such documents may include documents reasonably required for GST registration and/or registration of seller accounts on applicable online marketplaces.
3.3. The Client shall be responsible for providing complete, accurate, valid, current, and genuine information and documentation to the Company.
3.4. The Company shall not be responsible for delays, rejection, suspension, restriction, or cancellation of any registration or marketplace seller account arising from inaccurate, incomplete, invalid, expired, or misleading information supplied by the Client.
3.5. The Client acknowledges that registrations and seller accounts on third-party marketplaces may remain subject to the independent terms, policies, verification procedures, and requirements of the relevant marketplace.


4. PRODUCT ORDERS AND PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS

4.1. Where the Client receives an order through any applicable marketplace seller account, the Client shall notify the Company of such order in accordance with the Company's prescribed process.
4.2. Upon receipt of an applicable order, the Client shall be responsible for paying the applicable wholesale/product price to the Company in accordance with this Agreement.
4.3. The applicable wholesale price of each product shall be communicated by the Company along with the relevant product list or catalogue made available to the Client.
4.4. Product prices may be revised from time to time due to changes in procurement costs, product availability, supplier pricing, market conditions, logistics costs, or other relevant commercial factors.
4.5. Where the applicable price of a product changes from the price previously communicated in the product list, the Company shall notify the Client of the revised price as applicable.
4.6. The Client shall not assume that a previously communicated product price will remain permanently fixed unless expressly confirmed by the Company in writing.



5. ORDER NOTIFICATION AND PAYMENT TIMELINE

5.1. The Client shall notify the Company promptly upon receiving an order through any applicable marketplace.
5.2. Following notification of an order, the Client shall make the applicable payment to the Company within 12 hours from the time of order notification.
5.3. The Company shall process and dispatch the relevant order after receipt of the required payment from the Client, subject to product availability, operational requirements, marketplace requirements, and other applicable circumstances.
5.4. The Client acknowledges that timely payment is necessary for the Company to process and dispatch orders within the applicable operational timeframe.
5.5. Any delay in payment by the Client may result in a corresponding delay in order processing or dispatch.


6. ORDER DISPATCH

6.1. Upon receipt and confirmation of the applicable payment, Rigel Enterprises shall arrange for dispatch of the relevant product from the Company's location to the buyer's delivery location, subject to applicable operational conditions.
6.2. The time required for dispatch may vary depending upon the Client's membership plan, order volume, product availability, operational requirements, and other relevant circumstances.
6.3. Premium plan members may receive priority in order dispatch, subject to the terms applicable to the relevant membership plan.
6.4. Any estimated dispatch timeframe communicated by the Company shall be subject to circumstances affecting inventory, logistics, marketplace requirements, holidays, operational interruptions, and other factors beyond the Company's reasonable control.



7. FAILURE OR INABILITY TO MAKE ORDER PAYMENT

7.1. If the Client is unable to make the required payment for an order, the Client shall notify the Company promptly.
7.2. In such circumstances, the Parties shall mutually discuss and determine the appropriate course of action regarding the relevant order and payment.
7.3. The Company shall not be obligated to dispatch an order for which the required payment has not been received unless otherwise mutually agreed by the Parties.
7.4. Any arrangement made between the Parties under this section shall be specific to the relevant circumstances and shall not automatically modify the general payment obligations established under this Agreement.


8. CUSTOMER RETURNS

8.1. In the event that an end customer returns an order, the Company shall return to the Client the amount previously paid by the Client to the Company in respect of that particular order, subject to the terms and conditions applicable to the relevant transaction.
8.2. Where applicable, such amount shall be returned within 7 working days following the relevant return being processed and confirmed.
8.3. Alternatively, with mutual agreement between the Parties, the applicable amount may be adjusted against a subsequent order of the Client.
8.4. Any adjustment against a future order shall be made only pursuant to mutual discussion and agreement between the Parties.
8.5. The treatment of marketplace charges, penalties, deductions, shipping charges, or other amounts imposed independently by a marketplace shall remain subject to the applicable marketplace policies and the circumstances of the relevant transaction.


9. MARKETPLACE PAYMENT SETTLEMENT

9.1. Payments received through the applicable marketplace shall ordinarily be settled directly into the bank account selected by the Client.
9.2. Such settlement may occur within approximately **7 to 15 days following delivery of the products**, depending upon the settlement procedures and policies of the relevant marketplace.
9.3. The Company shall not be responsible for settlement delays caused by a marketplace, payment processor, banking institution, verification procedure, dispute, customer return, or other third-party process outside the Company's reasonable control.
9.4. The Client acknowledges that marketplace settlement timelines are determined by the relevant marketplace and may vary from transaction to transaction.


10. PRODUCT INFORMATION AND AVAILABILITY

10.1. The Company may provide the Client with lists of products that may be available for listing through the Client's marketplace seller accounts.
10.2. Product availability, pricing, specifications, and other commercial information may change from time to time.
10.3. The Client shall be responsible for ensuring that products are listed accurately on the relevant marketplace and in accordance with the marketplace's applicable rules and requirements.
10.4. The Company may update, modify, replace, or discontinue products made available to the Client where circumstances require such changes.


11. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

The Client agrees to:
11.1. Provide accurate, complete, valid, and genuine information and documentation to the Company.
11.2. Maintain valid and operational marketplace seller accounts.
11.3. Notify the Company promptly upon receiving applicable customer orders.
11.4. Make required product payments within the agreed 12-hour payment period.
11.5. Comply with the applicable terms, policies, and requirements of the relevant marketplace.
11.6. Avoid submitting false, misleading, fraudulent, or unauthorized information.
11.7. Cooperate with the Company where information or documentation is reasonably required for order processing.
11.8. Refrain from engaging in activities intended to manipulate, misuse, or abuse the Company's services or applicable marketplace systems.


12. FRAUDULENT OR FAKE ORDERS

12.1. The Company reserves the right to take appropriate action where it identifies or reasonably determines that the Client has engaged in the placement, generation, facilitation, or submission of fake, fraudulent, manipulated, or otherwise unauthorized orders.
12.2. Such conduct may result in suspension or termination of the Client's access to the Company's services.
12.3. The Company may also take such other action as may be available to it under applicable law or under the applicable marketplace rules.
12.4. The Client acknowledges that genuine commercial transactions are an essential requirement of the business relationship and agrees not to engage in activities intended to artificially generate orders, manipulate marketplace performance, or otherwise misuse the services provided by the Company.


13. CONFIDENTIALITY

13.1. The Parties acknowledge that this Agreement and information exchanged in connection with the business relationship may contain confidential and commercially sensitive information.
13.2. Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the contents of this Agreement and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior consent of the other Party, except where disclosure is:
a. Required by applicable law;
b. Required pursuant to an order or direction of a competent court or governmental authority;
c. Required for legitimate legal proceedings; or
d. Otherwise permitted under this Agreement.
13.3. Where a Party is legally required to disclose any portion of this Agreement pursuant to a court order or other lawful requirement, that Party shall, to the extent legally permissible, promptly notify the other Party of such requirement.
13.4. The confidentiality obligations contained herein shall not prevent either Party from making disclosures that are legally mandatory.


14. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY AND BUSINESS MATERIALS

14.1. Any product information, business materials, documents, lists, commercial information, procedures, or other materials supplied by the Company shall be used by the Client solely for legitimate purposes connected with the services contemplated under this Agreement.
14.2. The Client shall not knowingly misuse, reproduce, distribute, disclose, or commercially exploit confidential business information belonging to the Company except as reasonably required for the purposes of the Parties' business relationship or as otherwise authorized.


15. COMPLIANCE WITH APPLICABLE REQUIREMENTS

15.1. Each Party shall comply with the laws and regulations applicable to its respective activities under this Agreement.
15.2. The Client shall remain responsible for complying with applicable requirements governing the Client's marketplace seller accounts and activities conducted through those accounts.
15.3. Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as transferring the Client's independent legal or regulatory responsibilities to the Company unless expressly agreed in writing.


16. THIRD-PARTY MARKETPLACES

16.1. The Parties acknowledge that online marketplaces, payment processors, logistics providers, banks, and other third parties may impose their own independent terms, procedures, restrictions, and requirements.
16.2. The Company shall not be deemed to control the policies, decisions, processing times, account restrictions, payment settlement procedures, or other actions of independent third parties.
16.3. Where a matter is dependent upon a third-party marketplace or service provider, the applicable third-party policies and procedures may affect the performance and timing of the relevant transaction.


17. COMMUNICATIONS

17.1. The Parties shall communicate with each other through the contact details and communication channels designated by them for purposes of the business relationship.
17.2. The Client shall promptly communicate information concerning customer orders, payment requirements, returns, or other matters that may materially affect order processing.
17.3. The Parties shall make reasonable efforts to maintain accurate contact information throughout the duration of their business relationship.


18. NO UNAUTHORIZED REPRESENTATION

18.1. Neither Party shall represent that it has authority to bind the other Party to any third-party obligation unless such authority has been expressly granted.
18.2. The Client shall not represent itself as an employee, legal representative, partner, agent, or authorized representative of Rigel Enterprises except to the extent expressly authorized in writing.


19. TERM AND CONTINUATION

19.1. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue unless terminated or otherwise discontinued by mutual agreement of the Parties or in accordance with applicable law.
19.2. The continuation of individual services may remain subject to the applicable membership plan, commercial arrangements, marketplace requirements, and operational conditions communicated to the Client.


20. TERMINATION AND SUSPENSION

20.1. The Company may suspend access to its services where it reasonably determines that the Client has materially breached the terms of this Agreement, engaged in fraudulent or unauthorized activity, submitted fake orders, or otherwise misused the Company's services.
20.2. The Company may take such action as is reasonably necessary to protect its business, systems, commercial interests, or compliance obligations.
20.3. Termination or suspension shall not automatically extinguish payment obligations or other obligations that accrued prior to the effective date of termination or suspension.


21. LIMITATION RELATING TO THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

21.1. The Client acknowledges that the Company may depend upon third-party marketplaces, payment systems, logistics providers, banks, suppliers, and other external service providers.
21.2. Accordingly, delays, restrictions, suspensions, failures, or changes imposed by such third parties may affect the provision of services.
21.3. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a guarantee that an independent third party will approve, maintain, process, settle, deliver, or otherwise facilitate any particular transaction.


22. AMENDMENTS

22.1. Any material amendment to this Agreement shall be communicated to the Parties and, where required, documented in writing.
22.2. Product pricing, product availability, operational procedures, and similar commercial matters may be updated from time to time in accordance with applicable business requirements.
22.3. An amendment shall not be interpreted as retroactively modifying transactions already completed unless expressly agreed by the Parties.


23. SEVERABILITY

If any provision of this Agreement is determined by a competent authority to be invalid, unlawful, or unenforceable, such provision shall, to the extent possible, be interpreted or modified so as to preserve its lawful purpose. The remaining provisions of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect to the extent permitted by applicable law.


24. GOVERNING JURISDICTION

24.1. The Parties agree that any legal proceedings arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts having jurisdiction within the **National Capital Territory of Delhi**, subject to applicable law.
24.2. Nothing in this clause shall prevent either Party from exercising any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.


25. ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

By signing below, each Party confirms that it has read and understood the terms of this Agreement and agrees to abide by the obligations applicable to it. Each Party further confirms that the information supplied by it for the purposes of this Agreement is accurate to the best of its knowledge and that it is entering into this Agreement voluntarily. ---
