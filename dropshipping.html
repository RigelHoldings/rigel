Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of products can I sell?

We offers products across multiple categories including clothing, home décor, desktop accessories and mobile accessories. The product catalogue is managed by Rigel.

Do I have to purchase products in bulk?

No. You pay the wholesale price when a customer order is generated rather than purchasing large quantities in advance.

How long does the initial paperwork take?

Where GST registration and seller-account setup are required, the process can take up to around 15 to 20 days depending on registration and verification.

What happens if customer returns a product?

In case a product gets returned the price you've paid will be settled in your bank account in 3 days.
